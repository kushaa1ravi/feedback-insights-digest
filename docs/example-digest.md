### 1. Grocery list not syncing between devices

Bug · 6 mentions (24%) · average rating 1.5

Shared and multi-device grocery lists are not staying in step. Items added or ticked on one phone are missing or delayed on the other, and one customer lost a list after signing in on a new phone.

**Suggested next step:** Reproduce list sync across two signed-in devices on the latest release and fix it before the next update ships.

> “My husband and I share a grocery list but items I add on my phone never show up on his. We ended up buying milk twice.” (feedback #1, rated 1/5)

### 2. Cooking mode and quick planning praised

Praise · 5 mentions (20%) · average rating 4.8

Customers like the hands-free, step-by-step cooking mode and say that planning a week of meals is much faster than before.

**Suggested next step:** Keep cooking mode prominent and use these comments in store listings.

> “The step-by-step cooking mode is brilliant. Screen stays on and the timers start by themselves.” (feedback #2, rated 5/5)

### 3. Planner slow or crashing since update

Performance · 4 mentions (16%) · average rating 2.0

Since the latest update the weekly planner is slow to open, freezes when switching weeks and crashes for at least one customer.

**Suggested next step:** Profile planner loading on the latest release and compare it with the previous version.

> “Weekly planner takes 10-15 seconds to open after the last update. It used to be instant.” (feedback #3, rated 2/5)

### 4. Filter recipes by diet

Feature request · 4 mentions (16%) · average rating 3.5

Customers want to filter or hide recipes by dietary preference, such as vegetarian, vegan, egg-free and high-protein.

**Suggested next step:** Add diet filters to recipe search, starting with vegetarian and vegan.

> “Love the recipes but please add a vegetarian filter. Scrolling past chicken dishes every time is tiring.” (feedback #5, rated 4/5)

### 5. Features moved behind premium

Pricing · 3 mentions (12%) · average rating 2.3

Customers object to paying for things they expected to be free, such as longer meal plans and exporting a grocery list, and find premium expensive for what it adds.

**Suggested next step:** Review which features sit behind premium and test a cheaper yearly plan.

> “Why are meal plans longer than 3 days locked behind premium now? That was free last month.” (feedback #6, rated 2/5)
